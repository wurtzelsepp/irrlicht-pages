Wisp – Delete your data

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Wisp by Martin Scholz has no user accounts. Your progress is stored on your device; we do not keep data about you on our own servers. This is how you delete your data:

1. Progress on your device

  • Reset progress: in the game under "Options > Account > Reset progress". Stars, skins, best times and achievements are deleted; your purchase history and privacy choice are kept so that purchased items stay valid.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the game, or clear the app data in the Android settings under "Apps > Wisp > Storage". This removes all locally stored data, including purchase history and privacy choice. Google Play restores permanent purchases the next time you start the game.

2. Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you use Google Play Games: open the Google Play Games app and choose "Settings > Delete Play Games account & data". There you can delete Wisp's saved game.

3. Data held by ad partners

The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads. They process your advertising ID, approximate location, device and diagnostic data, ad interactions and information about in-app purchases.

  • Delete the advertising ID: in the Android settings under "Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID". The stored data can then no longer be linked to your device.
  • Withdraw consent: in the game under "Options > Legal > Privacy settings".
  • Request deletion: email mazemaker@web.de with the subject "Delete data – Wisp". We forward your request to Unity and confirm it to you. You can also contact Unity directly (Unity privacy policy).

4. What is kept

  • Purchases: orders and payments are managed by Google Play. Google keeps them according to its own rules and legal obligations; we do not store any payment details.
  • Emails to us: we delete your message once your request has been handled, unless legal retention periods apply (for example up to 6 years for business letters).
  • Ad partners: how long Unity keeps data is described in the Unity privacy policy.

More information is in our privacy policy.
