2. Overview

Wisp has no user account of its own and no servers of its own. We do not receive any personal data from you ourselves. Data is only processed

  • locally on your device (progress and settings),
  • by Google, if you use the online features of Google Play Games or make a purchase,
  • by advertising partners when advertising banners are shown (not with "Ad-free").

3. Local storage on your device

The game stores on your device: stars, purchase history (section 5), your answer in the consent dialog, bought and selected skins, best times and best runs ("ghost"), redeemed codes, achievements, daily maze streak, tutorial status and your settings. This data does not leave your device unless you enable cloud save (section 4).

The legal basis is § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (strictly necessary for the service you requested) and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. You delete your game progress under "Options > Account > Reset progress"; your purchase history and consent answer are kept so that your purchases stay valid. You delete all local data by uninstalling the game or clearing the app's storage in the Android settings.

4. Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games and use cloud save, leaderboards or achievements, Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google") processes your player ID, player name, saved progress including the purchase history, scores and unlocked achievements. Leaderboards are visible to other players, depending on your Google Play Games settings.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the features you chose). You can turn off cloud save in the options; you delete stored data in the Google Play Games app (Settings > Delete account and data). Information: Google privacy policy.

5. Purchases via Google Play

If you buy star packs, "All skins" or "Ad-free", Google processes the purchase and the payment. Google processes your payment and account data under its own responsibility. We only receive a confirmation of the purchase from Google (order number, product, time, status), but not your name or payment details. The game checks the purchase status with Google to unlock purchased content on your devices. To connect to Google Play, the game uses Unity In-App Purchasing by Unity Technologies; technical data (for example IP address and device information) may be transmitted to Unity in the process. The game stores the order number, product and date of each purchase as a purchase history in your progress and, if you use it, in the cloud save (section 4), so that purchases apply on all your devices and are not credited twice.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the contract) and Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR where we must retain order data under commercial and tax law (up to ten years).

6. Advertising

As long as you have not bought "Ad-free", the game shows a banner on menu screens. If you have consented to advertising, this is an ad banner; for this we use the ad mediation platform Unity LevelPlay (ironSource Mobile Ltd. / Unity Technologies) and the ad network Unity Ads. The mediation selects an ad network for each ad. If you decline, we do not start the ad SDK; instead, a notice of our own about "Ad-free" appears in the same place and at the same size, for which no data is collected or transmitted.

Data processed (only with consent): your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID), IP address, device and operating system information (for example model, language, screen size), identifiers assigned by the ad SDK (user IDs), app information and diagnostic data (for example load times and errors when loading ads), approximate location (derived from the IP address), interactions with ads (impressions, views, taps, clicks), information about in-app purchases (purchase history, without payment details) and your consent status.

Purposes: serving and billing ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention, measuring ad performance and personalised advertising based on your interests.

Legal basis: When you start the game for the first time, we ask you in a consent dialog; no ads are loaded until you answer. The game stores your choice on your device. Your consent is the legal basis for accessing information on your device (for example the advertising ID) and for the advertising (§ 25(1) TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without consent, the ad SDK is not started and no data is transmitted to ad partners.

Withdrawal: You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future under "Options > Legal > Privacy settings". After a withdrawal the ad banner disappears immediately, and from the next start of the game the ad SDK is no longer started. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in the Android settings (Google > Ads). You can request deletion of the data stored by the ad partners from us (section 12) or directly from Unity.

The providers partly process the data under their own responsibility. Information: Unity privacy policy.

7. Sharing maze codes and contact

When you share a maze code, the game opens your device's share menu. You decide what you share and with whom; the app you choose (for example a messenger) processes the message under its own privacy policy. We do not receive any data.

If you send us an email (for example with a question, a complaint or a withdrawal), we process your email address and the content of your message to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We delete the message once it has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply. Our mailbox is hosted by WEB.DE (1&1 Mail & Media GmbH, Germany).

8. Permissions

The game uses the following Android permissions: internet access (advertising, Google Play Games, purchases), vibration (haptic feedback, can be switched off in the options) and access to the advertising ID (advertising, see section 6). The game does not access contacts, photos, microphone, camera or your precise location.

9. Recipients and transfers to third countries

Recipients are the providers named in sections 4 to 6 and their service providers. Data may be transferred to countries outside the EU, in particular to the USA and Israel. For the USA there is an adequacy decision of the EU Commission (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) for certified companies; there is also an adequacy decision for Israel. Otherwise, the providers base the transfer on the EU Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

10. Storage period

Local data remains stored until you reset it or uninstall the game. Cloud saves remain with Google until you delete them. We only keep purchase data as long as statutory retention obligations apply. The storage period at the advertising partners is governed by their privacy policies.

11. Children

The game is not specifically directed at children under 13. Children under 16 may only consent to advertising with the consent of their parents or guardians (Art. 8 GDPR). Parents or guardians who believe that their child has consented without permission can withdraw the consent in the privacy settings or contact us.

12. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to withdraw consent given (Art. 7(3)).

Right to object: Where we process data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). You can end advertising at any time by withdrawing your consent or buying "Ad-free".

As we do not store any personal data ourselves, please also contact Google or the advertising partners directly about data held by them. We are happy to help you with this.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the member state of your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement.

13. No obligation to provide data, no automated decisions

You are not obliged to provide any data. Without consent you will not see ads from our ad partners, but a notice of our own about "Ad-free"; without Google Play Games, cloud save, leaderboards and online achievements are not available. There is no automated decision-making with legal effect for you (Art. 22 GDPR).

14. Changes

We will update this privacy policy if the game or the legal situation changes. You can always find the current version in the game under "Options > Legal".
